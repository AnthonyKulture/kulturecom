For a hotel on the French Riviera, direct bookings are mostly won on Google: the property's profile, the free booking links that show your own rate next to Booking.com's, and a website that answers travellers' specific questions better than the platforms do. For a holiday rental in Nice, Cannes or Monaco the route is different, because Google closes several of those doors to individual owners.

I work on both cases from Nice. Below, the hotel first, then the rental.

## How much of hotel booking is still direct in Europe?

A little over half. HOTREC's 2026 European Hotel Distribution Study, run by Roland Schegg (HES-SO Valais-Wallis) across 2,713 hotels and [published in September 2026](https://traveltomorrow.com/booking-and-expedia-account-for-85-of-europes-ota-market-hotrec-study-finds/), puts direct bookings at 51.3% and online travel agencies (OTAs) at 29.9%, up from 19.7% in 2013. Booking Holdings alone accounts for almost 70% of OTA bookings.

That "direct" figure lumps together phone, email, walk-ins and the website. What matters to a hotelier in Nice is the share they can win back at the moment a traveller compares online. That's where SEO and GEO come in.

## Can a hotel be cheaper on its own website than on Booking.com?

Yes, in France since 2015 and across Europe since 2024.

France's Macron law of 6 August 2015 gave hoteliers back the freedom to offer guests any discount or rate advantage, and any contrary clause in a platform contract is deemed unwritten. At EU level, the Commission designated Booking.com a gatekeeper under the Digital Markets Act on 13 May 2024. According to [the European Commission's factsheet](https://digital-markets-act.ec.europa.eu/factsheet-how-dma-ensures-businesses-using-bookingcom-are-free-set-their-prices-and-bookingcom-2026-09-28_en), since 14 November 2024 Booking.com must let properties offer different prices and conditions on other channels, their own website included. The same factsheet, updated in September 2026, adds that prices charged elsewhere no longer count towards eligibility for the Booking Sponsored Benefit programme or towards default ranking.

One exception: Monaco is not part of the European Economic Area, and French law does not apply there. A Monaco hotel should reread its contract before playing with rates.

My view: knocking 5% off the price doesn't always make people switch channel. A visible perk (breakfast, late checkout, parking, an upgrade when available) is easier to understand and harder to compare.

## How do you show your own rate on Google?

When someone searches for your hotel's name, Google shows a booking module with prices from several sellers. Your website can appear there for free through free booking links.

According to [Google's Hotel Center help](https://support.google.com/hotelprices/answer/10472393?hl=en), these links show your site or hotel name with the room rate for the chosen dates, lead to your booking page, cost nothing per click, and nobody can pay to rank higher in them. Google orders them using several signals, including user preference, the value offered, the landing page experience and the historical accuracy of the prices you send.

In practice, for an independent hotel:

1. Check whether your booking engine or channel manager is a Google connectivity partner. Google says your provider can set up these links for you.
2. Claim the hotel's Google Business Profile and make the name, address and phone number match the website.
3. Send accurate prices. A rate shown on Google that changes at checkout costs you ranking and trust.
4. Work on the landing page: the advertised price visible straight away, the room, the cancellation terms, a simple mobile checkout.

The [Google Business Profile guide for Nice](/en/blog/nice-google-business-profile-guide/) covers categories, photos and reviews.

## What about a holiday rental?

[Google Business Profile's eligibility rules](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en) exclude rental or for-sale properties such as vacation homes. An apartment let on Airbnb therefore can't have its own profile. Sales or leasing offices are still eligible: a concierge company or agency with premises where it meets clients can have one.

Vacation rental listings on Google follow the same logic. Google doesn't accept individual homeowners, peer-to-peer rentals, individual rooms or partial homes. A management company with fewer than 500 properties has to go through an approved connectivity provider.

For a single owner with two flats in Nice, the realistic route is:

- a simple, fast website with one page per property and a real booking engine, or at least an enquiry form;
- the furnished tourist rental registration number, which [Service-Public](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2043) requires in every rental listing (I put it on the website too);
- staying in touch with past guests, who often come back direct the following year if you keep the relationship going.

On cost, Airbnb's help centre says most hosts are moving to a single 15.5% service fee, with the old split-fee model (about 3% for the host) being phased out. That margin is what a website has to beat.

## Which pages does a hotel website need to rank?

Booking.com wins the generic searches ("hotel Nice"). Your site can win the specific ones, where travellers already know what they want. A few examples I test for properties in the area:

| Search | What it expects | Page to create |
|---|---|---|
| "sea view hotel Promenade des Anglais" | sea-facing rooms, real photos | one page per room category |
| "hotel with parking Old Nice" | parking, car access, price | a practical "Getting here and parking" page |
| "hotel near Palais des Festivals Cannes" | walking distance, congress dates | a page in English on the area and events |
| "apartment to rent Monaco Grand Prix" | view of the circuit, minimum stay | a seasonal page updated every year |
| "quiet family hotel Cimiez" | family rooms, garden | a "Family stays" page |

Pages that work answer in one sentence at the top ("A 4-minute walk from the Croisette"), then give the details. They also exist in French and Italian if your guests need it, each at its own URL, as covered in the article on [multilingual websites on the French Riviera](/en/blog/multilingual-website-french-riviera/).

Add structured data that describes the property (Schema.org `Hotel` or `LodgingBusiness` type, address, phone, coordinates, reception hours). The details are in the [LocalBusiness schema guide](/en/blog/localbusiness-schema-markup-guide/). In Cannes, the Palais des Festivals calendar (the Festival in May, MIPIM and the congresses) deserves its own page; I cover it in the [Cannes local SEO guide](/en/blog/seo-cannes-local-search/).

## How do AI assistants choose a hotel to recommend?

More and more travellers ask an AI. In BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US consumers, 45% had used ChatGPT or another AI tool to get local recommendations.

What to know as of November 2026:

- Booking.com and Expedia launched their apps in ChatGPT back in October 2025. When a traveller calls them up, they see those platforms' prices, and the booking is completed on their sites.
- Some tools also let hotels appear with their own rates. In March 2026 Lighthouse launched The Hotels Network app in ChatGPT: the traveller is sent to the hotel's website to book, on a flat subscription with no commission. I haven't used it; I mention it to show the door exists.
- Google is testing hotel booking in AI Mode. Announced in late August 2026, it is limited to the US, in English, with partners such as Booking.com, Expedia, Marriott and Hilton, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/27/googles-ai-mode-can-now-track-flight-prices-help-book-hotels-and-more/). I haven't found an announcement for France so far.

In regular answers, without an app, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity cite the properties they find across several sources: your site, your Google profile, reviews, local press, guides. Try questions such as "charming hotel with a terrace near the port of Nice" or "quiet boutique hotel in Monaco walking distance to the casino", and note who comes up and which pages get cited. The full method is in the article on [local GEO](/en/blog/local-geo-generative-engine-optimization/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a hotel show a lower price on its own website than on Booking.com?

Yes. In France, the 2015 Macron law bans rate parity clauses for hotels, and since November 2024 the Digital Markets Act has required Booking.com to let EEA properties set different prices on their own websites. Monaco, outside the EEA, calls for a check of the contract.

### Are Google's free booking links really free?

Yes. Google charges neither for signing up nor for clicks, and nobody can pay to rank higher in them. You go through Google Hotel Center, often via the hotel's booking engine or channel manager, which sends rates and availability.

### Can an Airbnb rental in Nice have a Google Business Profile?

No. Google excludes vacation homes from Google Business Profile. A concierge company or agency that meets clients in an office can, however, create a profile for that office.

### Do you need to leave Booking.com or Airbnb to succeed with direct bookings?

No. The platforms bring guests you wouldn't have found on your own. The aim is to win back travellers who already know you or who are looking for something specific, and to bring back direct those who enjoyed their stay.
