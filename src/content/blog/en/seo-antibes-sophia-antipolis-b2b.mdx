SEO in Antibes and SEO in Sophia Antipolis need separate plans, even though the two markets sit side by side. In Antibes and Juan-les-Pins you sell to residents and visitors searching on Google Maps; in Sophia Antipolis you sell to companies that compare suppliers over several weeks, and increasingly ask ChatGPT for a shortlist.

If your business serves both, you need two sets of pages, one well-configured Google profile and a consistent presence in the sources AI engines read. Here is how I approach it.

## Why do Antibes and Sophia Antipolis need two SEO strategies?

The customers, the searches and the time it takes to decide have almost nothing in common.

Antibes is the second-largest town in the Alpes-Maritimes, with 77,637 residents, ahead of Cannes (74,350), according to the [Insee 2023 reference populations](https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/fichier/8680740/PopRef2023_dep06_ALPES-MARITIMES.pdf) published in January 2026. Juan-les-Pins is not a separate town: it's a district of Antibes, even though people search for it as if it were.

Sophia Antipolis isn't a town either. The [Sophia Antipolis urban community](https://www.agglo-sophiaantipolis.fr/mon-agglo/1-agglo-1-technopole-24-communes/sophia-antipolis-1ere-technopole-deurope) describes it as a technology park of 2,500 companies, founded in 1969 and spread over five towns: Valbonne, Biot, Mougins, Vallauris and Antibes. So part of the park is in Antibes, which blurs the map a little more.

## Local SEO in Antibes and Juan-les-Pins: where to start?

With the Google Business Profile, because that's what appears in the map. Google ranks local results on three factors, set out in its [help page on local ranking](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en): relevance (how well the profile matches the search), distance (between the business and the searcher) and prominence (how well known the business is).

Distance matters a lot in Antibes because the town is spread out. Someone in La Fontonne won't necessarily see the same map as someone in Old Antibes. You can't move your address, but you can work on the other two factors:

- a precise primary category ("Ice cream shop" rather than "Restaurant", "Electrician" rather than "Construction company");
- accurate hours all year, with seasonal hours if you only open in summer in Juan-les-Pins;
- recent reviews that mention the service and the area, never requested in exchange for a discount;
- photos of the real place, shopfront and dining room;
- the same name, address and phone number on your site, Google, Bing Places, Apple and directories.

The [Google Business Profile guide for Nice](/en/blog/nice-google-business-profile-guide/) covers every setting, and it applies to Antibes as is.

### Neighbourhood pages, only when they're useful

People search by area: Old Antibes, Port Vauban, Juan-les-Pins, Cap d'Antibes, La Fontonne, Les Semboules. A page per area makes sense when it says something specific to that area. An estate agency can describe the Cap d'Antibes market and the Semboules market, which have little in common. A locksmith who duplicates the same page six times with a different district name is building doorway pages, which Google treats as spam.

### The season and the jazz festival

Juan-les-Pins runs on summer, and on Jazz à Juan in particular: the 65th edition took place from 9 to 19 July 2026 in the Pinède Gould, according to the festival's site. A restaurant, hotel or bar in Juan should keep a "Jazz à Juan" page at the same URL year after year, updated each spring with dates, hours and the walking distance to the Pinède. The editorial calendar method is the same as in the article on [SEO in Cannes](/en/blog/seo-cannes-local-search/).

## B2B SEO in Sophia Antipolis: who searches for what?

In Sophia, the person searching is rarely the person who signs. An engineer spots a supplier, their manager compares three names, procurement approves. Each of them may type a different query, or ask an AI.

B2B searches are longer and more technical: "cybersecurity integrator SME Alpes-Maritimes", "IP lawyer for startups Sophia Antipolis", "corporate seminar catering Valbonne". Volume is low and the value of each client is high. A page that answers one of those searches precisely is worth more than a generic article ranking for a broad keyword.

What works, in my view:

1. One page per service and per type of client ("Accounting for deeptech startups", "Hiring software engineers"), using the vocabulary people in that trade actually use.
2. Verifiable references: client names when they agree, sectors, project types, certifications.
3. An English version, because many companies in the park work in English. The setup is covered in the [multilingual website guide](/en/blog/multilingual-website-french-riviera/).
4. An "About" page with the real people, their background and their LinkedIn profiles.

### Your address in Sophia Antipolis: the Google profile trap

Many Sophia companies start out in an incubator, a business nursery or a coworking space. The [Google Business Profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en) rule out profiles for virtual offices, and only accept a coworking office if it has clear signage, you receive customers there and staff are present during opening hours. A company that doesn't receive anyone should set itself up as a service-area business, with the address hidden.

For the address itself, use the real town (Valbonne, Biot, Mougins…) everywhere, written identically, and mention Sophia Antipolis in your copy. The same guidelines forbid adding a place or a keyword to the business name: "Dupont Conseil" stays "Dupont Conseil", not "Dupont Conseil Sophia Antipolis AI expert".

## How does ChatGPT pick a B2B supplier?

From what it finds about you online, and B2B buyers already rely on it. In its [2026 AI search report](https://learn.g2.com/g2-2026-ai-search-insight-report), G2 surveyed 1,076 B2B decision-makers in March 2026: 51% start their software research with an AI chatbot more often than with Google, and 80% still use Google at some point in the process. Two caveats: the study covers software purchases, and G2 itself sells visibility to software vendors. The overall direction still matters for a tech company in Sophia.

To be cited when a buyer asks "which engineering firm in Sophia Antipolis for an embedded project?", your name needs to come up in several sources that AI engines read, with the same description:

- your site, with clear, dated expertise pages;
- your LinkedIn company page, with the same description of what you do;
- the directories and platforms of your sector (software review sites for a vendor, professional directories for a firm);
- local business press and trade press, and the pages of events where you speak;
- `Organization` structured data on your home page. [Google's documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization?hl=en) recommends properties such as `legalName`, `address`, `sameAs` (your official profiles), `vatID` or `numberOfEmployees`.

For a shop in Antibes, the same principles go through reviews, local guides and `LocalBusiness` markup, explained in the [LocalBusiness schema guide](/en/blog/localbusiness-schema-markup-guide/). The method for testing what AI engines answer is in the article on [local GEO](/en/blog/local-geo-generative-engine-optimization/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Should my Google profile say Antibes or Juan-les-Pins?

The address must be your real postal address, so the town of Antibes. Mention Juan-les-Pins in the profile description, on your site and in your review replies, but don't add the district to the business name: Google's guidelines forbid it.

### Does a Sophia Antipolis startup need a Google profile?

Only if it receives clients at a signed address with staff present. Otherwise it sets up as a service-area business or skips the profile, and puts its effort into expertise pages, LinkedIn and sector sources, which carry more weight in B2B.

### Does B2B SEO in Sophia Antipolis take longer than local SEO?

Usually, yes. B2B searches have little volume and decisions take weeks, so results show up as qualified enquiries rather than traffic. A well-configured Google profile in Antibes can bring in calls sooner.

### How do I know if ChatGPT recommends my business?

Ask it your customers' questions, in French and English, several times, and note who comes up and which sources it cites. Do the same in Perplexity and Gemini. The cited sources tell you where you need to be present.
